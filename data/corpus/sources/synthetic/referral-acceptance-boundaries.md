# SYNTHETIC: Referral documentation boundaries

SYNTHETIC TRAINING DOCUMENT. Project-authored on 2026-10-03, version 1.
The Kestrel training service is fictional. This policy contains no real referral,
patient identifier, professional contact or clinical triage instruction.

## Distinguish the events

A referral being requested, transmitted, acknowledged, accepted and completed
describes different events. The fictional record uses only the event supported
by the exercise material. Transmission does not imply acceptance; acceptance
does not imply that an assessment, investigation or treatment has occurred.

## Required source context

Record the stated purpose and the source of the request. Identify the receiving
role and requested action only when those details are available. Retain any
supplied limitation or outstanding question. Do not manufacture a service,
appointment, urgency classification or completion date.

## Negative evidence

A missing acknowledgement is not evidence of refusal. A blank outcome field is
not evidence that the referral was completed without findings. The existence
of a guideline in the corpus does not itself establish that a referral meets
that guideline's criteria.

## Review and closure

The reviewer records unresolved documentation questions and the supplied owner
of each question. Closure needs an explicit source for the reported outcome.
Where two sources disagree, both remain attributed until a resolution is
documented. This fictional process policy supplies no treatment or medicine
recommendation and does not implement an application workflow.
