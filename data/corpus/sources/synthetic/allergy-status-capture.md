# SYNTHETIC: Allergy-status documentation vocabulary

SYNTHETIC TRAINING DOCUMENT. Project-authored on 2026-10-03, version 1.
This is a fictional Kestrel training policy with no patient records or external
clinical authority. It defines documentation distinctions, not clinical advice.

## Distinct states

The training vocabulary distinguishes a reported reaction, a reviewed reaction
history, an explicitly supplied negative history, and unavailable information.
These states are not interchangeable. An empty field means that information
was not supplied to the exercise; it does not mean that no reaction exists.

## Recording a supplied statement

Preserve the reported substance, the described reaction and the information
source when those details are present. If timing or severity is absent, mark
it as unavailable. Do not supply a more specific mechanism, severity grade or
diagnostic label because it is commonly associated with that substance.

Separate a source's assertion from the reviewer's conclusion. A draft may say
that a source reports a reaction without claiming that a clinical assessment
has confirmed its mechanism. If two supplied records differ, preserve both
assertions and flag the discrepancy for review.

## Negative documentation evidence

Do not infer tolerance from the absence of a reaction in a document. Do not
replace an unknown history with a negative-history phrase. Do not infer that
a therapy is appropriate because a particular allergy is not listed. This
fictional vocabulary cannot establish a contraindication or rule one out.

## Review record

Record the source and scope of any supplied review. An acknowledgement of the
draft is not itself evidence that a reaction history was assessed. Corrections
identify the supporting information and preserve a traceable distinction
between the original report and the amended description.

## Limits

No drug names, real reaction histories, test results or patient identifiers are
provided. Clinical interpretation requires an applicable external reference and
qualified review; this document only supports accurate field wording.
