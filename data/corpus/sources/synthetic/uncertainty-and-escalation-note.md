# SYNTHETIC: Uncertainty and escalation note

SYNTHETIC TRAINING DOCUMENT. Project-authored on 2026-10-03, version 1.
This reference describes a fictional Kestrel documentation exercise. It has no
patient records, real contact details or clinical escalation timetable.

## Describe the uncertainty

The fictional note identifies the unresolved question, the supplied information
that bears on it and the information that is missing. It distinguishes a lack
of information from contradictory information. Neither condition is resolved
by writing a confident summary without supporting evidence.

## Keep claims within their sources

Preserve the population, setting, route, time period and conditions attached to
an external statement. If those limits do not match the exercise, record the
gap rather than transferring the conclusion. A similar-sounding medicine or
procedure does not supply missing information for a different one.

An absence of interaction data is not a compatibility finding. A document that
does not state a dose cannot supply one through calculation or recollection.
This synthetic note provides neither dose values nor clinical interaction
claims; it explains how to document that evidence is insufficient.

## Escalation record

Record the role to which the open question was directed only when that role is
supplied. Keep prepared, communicated and answered statuses distinct. Do not
invent a communication time, acknowledgement, service-level target or response.
An unanswered request remains unanswered in the training record.

## Completion boundary

The exercise can close a documentation question only when a resolution and its
source are explicitly supplied. Closing the documentation item does not imply
that an intervention was ordered, administered or clinically appropriate.
Clinical urgency and emergency action are outside the scope of this reference.
